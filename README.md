# Spreads, Liquidity Timing and Order-Book Gaps in Chinese A-Shares

**Tianzuo Hu, Cornell University**

An empirical study of 3-second Level-2 limit-order-book snapshots and 15-second trade records for
five anonymised Chinese A-shares (4–6 January 2021, 71,085 snapshots).

📄 **Full report:** [`report/report.pdf`](report/report.pdf)

## Overview

The project builds a validated intraday panel from raw order-book and trade files. It then studies
four microstructure questions:

1. **Bid–ask spread.** What does the spread distribution look like, and how do the two best quotes
   move when the spread widens?
2. **Intraday liquidity.** When during the day does trading happen, and how does one-minute
   turnover relate to the size of one-minute returns?
3. **Trade location.** Where does the last traded price sit relative to the five-level book?
4. **Order-book gaps.** How often do new limit orders appear at previously empty price levels
   ("free-hole additions"), and how much volume is queued ahead of them?

## Key findings

| Topic | Result |
|---|---|
| Spread | Large-tick (low-priced) stocks sit at a 1-tick spread 83–98% of the time; high-priced stocks have median spreads of 2–4 ticks. |
| Spread widening | 91–100% of widenings are one-sided: only one best quote moves outward. |
| Intraday timing | The first 30 minutes carry 26–29% of daily turnover (uniform benchmark: 12.7%). The correlation between \|1-min return\| and turnover is 0.34–0.37. |
| Trade location | Fresh prints occur at the best bid or best ask 80–99% of the time. The deep-book tails seen with forward-filled prices are mostly caused by stale prices. |
| Order-book gaps | 17,925 free-hole additions, 95% of them in the two stocks whose displayed books have empty ticks. Level 2 is the most common level. The median size in front is 2,100 shares. |

A single variable, the **relative tick size** (tick ÷ price), explains most of the
cross-sectional differences.

## Methods at a glance

- **pandas pipeline:** `groupby` features per stock-day (cumulative volume, returns, 30-snapshot
  rolling volatility); `resample` into right-closed one-minute bars; a left merge of trades onto
  snapshots with a one-to-one check.
- **Exact price arithmetic:** all price comparisons are done on integer ticks.
- **Built-in checks:** book monotonicity, row counts, merge cardinality, cross-checks of the
  one-minute bars against an independent construction, and a unit test for the free-hole
  detector. All are written as assertions, so the script stops if any check fails.

## Reproducing

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python src/analysis.py --data <data folder> --out <output folder>
```

The data folder must contain `snapshot/{date}/{id}.csv` and `trade/{date}/{id}.csv`.

> **Data are not included in this repository.**
